          Personal reading tool. Native RSS where available, Google News RSS where not; official releases come straight from each office's own site (the Governor, Attorney General, Comptroller, Assembly Speaker, Senate Majority Leader and Mayor); Senate members' releases come through Google News. Refreshes every 30 minutes. Local outlets across the state (their own dot color) come in through their own feeds; the Latest tab hides them unless you turn that off or choose a region or outlet. Regions are the state's ten economic development regions: a local outlet's stories go to its home region, a larger outlet's to the regions whose places the story names. Every article is its own row; "+N related stories" under a row lists other outlets' articles on the same subject, matched by headline, not copies of it.
